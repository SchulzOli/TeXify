# Quarterly Report Q3 2026

## Summary

The third quarter was **successful**: revenue grew by 12 %, customer
satisfaction is at *4.6 out of 5*. Two major projects were completed and a
third one started on schedule.

## Key figures

| Metric                   |      Q2 |      Q3 |  Change |
|:-------------------------|--------:|--------:|--------:|
| Revenue (k€)             |   1,240 |   1,389 |   +12 % |
| New customers            |      38 |      51 |   +34 % |
| Support tickets          |     412 |     365 |   −11 % |
| Avg. response time (h)   |     5.2 |     3.8 |   −27 % |

## Highlights

- Go-live of the customer portal at *Example Ltd.*
- Automated tests introduced for every product team
- ISO 27001 certification passed

## Risks

> Dependence on a single key account is still 31 % of revenue and should drop
> significantly next year.
