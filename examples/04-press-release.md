# Sample Association launches free writing workshop for volunteers

**Leipzig.** From November the Sample Association Leipzig offers a free
writing workshop. Volunteers learn to produce invitations, minutes and
donation receipts quickly and consistently.

“Many associations spend hours formatting documents. That time is missing for
the actual work,” says chair Jordan Sample. The workshop is based on freely
available templates that turn plain text files into professional PDFs.

The workshop runs on four evenings at the Town Hall. Registration is open
until 31 October at workshop@example.org.
